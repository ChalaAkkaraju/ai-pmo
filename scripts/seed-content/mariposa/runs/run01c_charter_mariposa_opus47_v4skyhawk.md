# Project Charter — Mariposa Wind Farm Phase 1

## 1. Project identification

- **Project name:** Mariposa Wind Farm Phase 1
- **Project ID:** NW-REN-2511
- **Client:** Mariposa Renewables Holdings (IPP)
- **Contract type:** Fixed-price
- **Contract value:** $148.0 million
- **Effective date:** 22 May
- **Target substantial completion date:** 30 November, year+1
- **Sponsor (Northwood):** VP Renewables
- **Project Director:** L. Chen
- **Project Manager:** J. Okafor

## 2. Overview

EPC delivery of a 320 MW greenfield onshore wind generation facility comprising 80 single-OEM turbines at 4.0 MW each, 110 m hub height, with associated collection system, substation, and grid interconnection. Northwood designs, procures and constructs the turbines, foundations, access roads, 34.5 kV collection system, substation with step-up to 230 kV, and approximately 3-mile interconnection to the existing utility line. Client operates under a power purchase agreement and is targeting energisation by 30 December year+1 to qualify for a higher federal tax-credit tier.

## 3. Objectives

1. Deliver 320 MW nameplate wind generation (80 turbines × 4.0 MW), verified through commissioning power test, by 30 November year+1.
2. Achieve grid interconnection energisation no later than 30 December year+1 to secure client's higher tax-credit tier.
3. Deliver at or above 9.5% margin with cost variance within ±2% of the $134.0M approved budget.
4. Complete commissioning, performance testing, operator training and as-built handover by Substantial Completion.
5. Zero lost-time injuries across construction.

## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In scope
- Detailed civil and electrical design for turbines, foundations, access roads, collection system, substation, and interconnection.
- Procurement of 80 wind turbine generators from single vetted OEM, transformers, switchgear, MV cabling.
- Civil construction: site grading, foundation works, on-site access roads, crane pads.
- Electrical construction: 34.5 kV collection system, substation, step-up to 230 kV, ~3-mile interconnection to existing utility line.
- Commissioning, performance testing, operator training, as-built documentation.
- 24-month warranty.

### 4.2 Out of scope
- Land lease management (client).
- Environmental permitting (client).
- Avian / migratory bird study (client).
- Transmission line beyond point of interconnection (utility-led).
- Road improvements outside site boundary (county DOTs).
- O&M services post handover.
