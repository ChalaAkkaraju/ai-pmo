## 8. Lessons learned — headlines from companion synthesis

Five lessons confirmed at closeout, drawn from the companion Lessons-Learned Synthesis (`run29_lessons_learned_mariposa.md` — referenced; full content is internal-only unless client-facing lessons are pre-approved). A sixth lesson is provisional pending warranty-tail evidence.

1. **Geotechnical investigation grid density on variable-terrain greenfield wind sites should exceed standard greenfield grid at proposal stage.** Evidence: Mariposa I-021 / R-009 three-position localised-and-contained variance arc at Plot B — empirically identical to Skyhawk Plot B NW quadrant. Two-of-two greenfield realisations following identical containment pattern; Riverside (brownfield) remains the structural exception. Owner function: Engineering Standards.

2. **The four-frame commercial dynamics discipline preserves margin on client-driven mid-construction scope additions.** Evidence: Mariposa CO-001 at 8.2% post-discipline margin vs Riverside CO-003 at 6.7% pre-discipline margin — 1.5 percentage-point margin protection on the closed CO event; first portfolio-validated application at renewables scale. Owner function: Commercial / Contract Standards.

3. **The 30-day SC-to-energisation buffer is structurally sufficient against mid-construction shocks on tax-credit-deadline projects.** Evidence: Mariposa buffer survived three material in-construction events (I-021 sub-grade Week 27, I-028 weather Week 49, Chain E float consumption Week 52) without erosion; SC on contractual date; energisation forecast within deadline. Owner function: Commercial / Proposal Standards.

4. **R5-pooled weather contingency sized against erection-season-climate-band expected stand-down windows works.** Evidence: I-028 4-day stand-down absorbed cleanly within R5 reserve; remaining erection season Weeks 50–70 saw no further multi-day events; 90% of R5 allocation intact at SC. Owner function: Project Methodology Standards.

5. **For tax-credit-tier-verification dispatch integration on IPP projects, proposal-stage scoping should explicitly clarify whether SCADA scope ends at plant control or extends to client portfolio OPCO.** Evidence: I-026 / CO-001 — asked at proposal stage, this would have priced the integration into base contract rather than as a CO, eliminating the 1.3 percentage-point Week 52 margin compression entirely. Owner function: Commercial / Proposal Standards.

**Provisional lesson (pending warranty-tail evidence).** Single-OEM concentration risk on fleet projects above 50 units is testable through FAT-discipline + lead-unit-erection-as-precedent + monthly financial-health monitoring + fleet performance-test contingency window of 7–10 working days. Mariposa's full Week 18 → Week 75 execution arc validates this through SC; the 24-month warranty tail provides further evidence on the firm-level standard practice recommendation.

## 10. Conventions used

Five patterns this document follows, for traceability against the Northwood Skyhawk worked-example convention:

1. **Objective-by-objective binary status with quantitative supporting metrics.** Charter §3's five objectives are the report's spine; each carries a Met / Partially met / Not met status at headline level with quantitative detail in supporting columns. Objective 2 (energisation by 30 December year+1) is reported as Met-on-forecast with explicit hedging on the final Week 82 confirmation; the discipline avoids both premature settlement and false uncertainty.

2. **Risk register closeout status with three categories.** Realised / Mitigated / Not Materialised, each with cited evidence (contingency consumption against R1–R5 allocations, variance section reference, change-order ID, or issue-log closure). The three-category framing prevents the "all risks managed successfully" closeout summary that obscures which risks actually appeared and which did not.

3. **Audience-aware disclosure discipline.** §1 Executive Summary is client-and-Sponsor-facing headline; §3 Financial Summary is full-detail for Sponsor and portfolio dashboard with no branch-level detail to client beyond contractually required disclosure; §8 Lessons Learned quotes only headlines from the synthesis with the full synthesis internal-only unless client-facing lessons are pre-approved. The disclosure boundary is operational, not editorial.

4. **Lessons-learned cross-reference, not duplication.** §8 quotes five confirmed headline lessons plus one provisional from the companion Lessons-Learned Synthesis; the synthesis carries the full content with evidence base and firm-level recommendations. The two documents are bidirectional cross-references — closeout cites synthesis sections; synthesis cites closeout §7 risk register status as a primary evidence source.

5. **Follow-on and handover items separated from outcome items.** §9 collects operational handover, Phase 2 prospect, portfolio-level transfer, and documentation archive items — forward-looking dimensions distinct from the outcome reporting in §1–§8. The separation makes the operational handover actionable. Forward-looking items dependent on events not yet occurred (energisation Week 82, Phase 2 prospect, warranty events, follow-on adoption status) carry inline italic hedging.
