## 3. Notes for PM

**Risks considered and excluded from the Closing-state register, with rationale:**

- **Warranty-tail single-OEM concentration as standalone risk** — folded into R-001 residual exposure rather than discrete entry. The 24-month warranty period exposure on single-OEM concentration is materially the same risk class as R-001 with reduced probability and impact at SC; opening a discrete warranty-tail register entry would duplicate management content. Standard Northwood practice is to carry R-001 into the warranty-period register with closeout-state response language.
- **Grid energisation event itself (Week 80-82) as standalone risk** — folded into R-002 and R-008 residual triggers rather than separate entry. The energisation event is the consummation of both risks' mitigation arcs; opening a discrete energisation-event risk would duplicate trigger language. If anomaly surfaces between SC and Week 82, R-002 and R-008 triggers fire.
- **Post-SC warranty support setup risk** — operational hand-off item managed through warranty workflow rather than risk register. The $0.85M residual budget covers grid energisation + warranty support setup + project close-out activities through Week 82; tracked via Variance Analyst final report and project accounting, not risk register.
- **OEM PO warranty enforcement at 24-month tail** — deferred to warranty-period register per standard Northwood practice; not yet relevant at SC. R-001 carries forward as the warranty-tail entry point.
- **CO-001 SCADA OPCO acceptance residual technical risk** — closed at I-030 remediation Week 66 and OPCO acceptance test pass. No residual; CO-001 integration complete at mechanical completion Week 65.
- **Lessons-learned evidence as risk** — not a risk; the five confirmed and one provisional lesson are inputs to the Lessons-Learned Synthesiser per the Iteration-3 lessons-confirmed framework. Pattern elevation candidates are inputs to the Portfolio Risk Reviewer Iteration-3 review.

**Risks that belong on the client's side of the table (per rule 9):**

- **Environmental permitting closure and warranty-period avian compliance** — client-owned per charter §4.2; Northwood-side R-003 closed at SC with Officer Whitlock final compliance call Week 76. Any warranty-period avian compliance question belongs with client.
- **Land lease management and warranty-period landowner relationships** — client-owned per charter §4.2; R-012 closed Not Materialised at SC with no opposition signals across 18-month execution. Any warranty-period landowner issues are client-side.
- **Transmission line beyond point of interconnection** — utility-owned per charter §4.2; R-008 closed for Northwood's interconnection-study dependency at SC and does not extend to line availability during operations.
- **PPA commercial terms and tax-credit-tier qualification mechanics at grid energisation** — client-owned; Northwood's commitment was the 30 December year+1 energisation date, forecast intact at Week 82. The CO-001 SCADA portfolio integration is the dispatch-verification enabler; mechanism qualification remains with client and their tax counsel.
- **Client OPCO operational integration and warranty-period SCADA portfolio operations** — client-side; Northwood delivered the integration scope through CO-001 with OPCO acceptance Week 66; operational stewardship transfers to client at SC.
