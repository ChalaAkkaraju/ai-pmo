## 3. Notes for PM

**Risks considered and excluded from the Northwood-side register, with rationale:**

- **Currency exposure on imported turbines** — excluded because charter §1/§7 treats the OEM PO at a committed dollar value; if the vetted OEM transacts in foreign currency, hedging would belong in the procurement contract terms, not the project risk register. `[CONFIRM WITH PM: confirm OEM PO currency and hedging arrangement before excluding permanently]`
- **Geotechnical surprise at substation site** — folded into R-009 (general site-conditions variance) rather than carried as a separate risk; substation pad represents <1% of foundation positions and is unlikely to be a discrete driver.
- **SCADA cybersecurity** — excluded at Week 0 because charter scope treats SCADA as Northwood-supplied integration into client's plant SCADA architecture; cybersecurity hardening sits within WBS 8.3 deliverables. Add to register if a client cybersecurity requirement emerges that exceeds standard Northwood SCADA practice.
- **OEM warranty enforcement at 24-month tail** — excluded from this register because the risk realises post-Substantial Completion and belongs in the warranty-period risk register, not the execution-period register. Carry forward to closeout.
- **Liquidated damages exposure as a standalone risk** — excluded because LD exposure is a *consequence* of R-002 (tax-credit deadline) and R-001 (OEM slip) realising, not an independent risk. Tracked through the energisation-buffer mechanism in R-002.

**Risks that belong on the client's side of the table (per rule 9):**

- **Environmental permitting and avian study completion in time to support mobilisation** — client-owned per charter §4.2 Out of scope. Northwood-side exposure is limited to schedule-knock-on if client permitting slips; that exposure is managed through stakeholder engagement with the Client Lead rather than as a Northwood risk.
- **Land lease management and landowner relationships** — client-owned per charter §4.2. The R-012 stakeholder risk on organised opposition is a Northwood operational concern through community-engagement support, but the contractual exposure on land-lease integrity is client-side.
- **Transmission line beyond point of interconnection** — utility-owned per charter §4.2.
- **Road improvements outside site boundary** — county DOT-owned per charter §4.2 (distinct from the within-scope rural-road *upgrades* covered by R-004).
- **PPA commercial terms and tax-credit-tier qualification mechanics** — client-owned. Northwood's commitment is the 30 December year+1 energisation date; the tax-credit qualification mechanism itself sits with the client and their tax counsel.
