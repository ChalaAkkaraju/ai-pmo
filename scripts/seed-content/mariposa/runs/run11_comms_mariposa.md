## 3. Message templates by audience segment

Four audience segments organise the message-template inventory: client side, regulators, community and press, internal Northwood. Each segment has a calibrated tone and three to five standing message types.

### 3.1 To the client side (Client Lead, Client Executive Sponsor)

**Tone:** *delivery confidence with disciplined honesty about the tax-credit deadline*. Energisation by 30 December year+1 is the single point of alignment; every status conversation references the energisation critical path. Northwood is the EPC lead; the client retains land-lease, permitting, and PPA-commercial ownership.

**Standing message types:**

- **Monthly written status report to the Client Lead.** Two-page format. Schedule status against milestones (charter §6) with explicit position against the 30-day SC-to-energisation buffer, cost status against approved budget (no branch-level disclosure), risks updated since last report, decisions needed from client, next-period plan. Sign-off: PM. *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: confirm two-page format with client at kickoff])*
- **Weekly informal voice call with the Client Lead.** No agenda. Used to surface emerging issues on OEM cadence, road-upgrade progress, and permit posture before they become formal. 20–30 minutes.
- **Biweekly site walk with the Client Lead and Construction Manager** (post-mobilisation). Visual progress, safety observations, items needing client decision.
- **Quarterly direct touch with the Client Executive Sponsor.** Triggered by quarterly cadence or by any event with material commercial or schedule visibility (energisation-buffer erosion, OEM tranche failure, organised opposition). Project Director owns. *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: confirm whether Mariposa has a distinct executive sponsor])*
- **Immediate escalation on any event that could move the energisation date.** No fixed cadence; trigger-driven per §4. Always written, always within 24 hours of internal Northwood decision.

### 3.2 To regulators (Permitting Authority, Utility Coordinator, EPA Region)

**Tone:** *we know the rules and we meet them*. Permit-driven dates are treated as fixed; the schedule is built around them, not the other way round. No surprises, no editorial, no advocacy.

**Standing message types:**

- **Monthly compliance touch with the assigned wildlife agency officer.** Permit compliance status, upcoming submission dates, any in-construction events that touched permit thresholds. Begins once officer is assigned at permit issuance. 30 minutes. *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: officer assignment pending permit issuance])*
- **Monthly coordination call with the Utility Coordinator.** Interconnection study status, energisation scheduling, system protection coordination, milestone dates against 30 Dec year+1 hard deadline. Begins at contract effective date through energisation.
- **Formal permit submissions and utility submissions on schedule.** Always on or ahead of due date. Acknowledgement-required submissions tracked separately. QA/QC Lead supports PM.
- **Pre-test procedure agreement with permitting authority and utility.** Window before performance-test commencement *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: confirm pre-test agreement window per WBS 9.6 and utility scheduling])*. Procedure-change negotiation handled through assigned officers; formal change requires written agreement.
- **EPA notification (triggered only).** Used only if the wildlife agency escalates an event to federal oversight, or if any environmental incident crosses a federal threshold. Sponsor owns.

### 3.3 To community and press (local landowner group, regional press, county councils)

**Tone:** *transparency with calm framing, client-led*. Community engagement is client-led with Northwood support; Northwood does not engage the community or press directly. County councils are political bodies briefed through the County Roads Leads, who are the operational interface.

**Standing message types:**

- **Quarterly community newsletter (via client communications lead).** Content covers construction progress, upcoming visible work (heavy-haul movements, tower erection sequence, noise events), wildlife-mitigation actions, energisation timeline. Northwood drafts technical content; client communications lead finalises and distributes. *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: confirm community-engagement split with client at kickoff])*
- **48-hour complaint response procedure.** Complaints route through client to Northwood; Northwood responds with factual answer within 48 hours; complex complaints requiring on-site investigation get a holding response in 24 hours and full response within 72 hours. Construction Manager owns.
- **Press enquiry response (triggered only).** All press requests route through client communications lead. Northwood does not comment to press directly. Northwood provides factual background only on client request, in writing, with quote review.
- **County council briefings (triggered).** Briefed via County Roads Leads ahead of any council session where the project may be discussed; written summary on request. Project Director owns. *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: confirm whether one or both county councils require direct briefings])*

### 3.4 To internal Northwood (Sponsor, Project Director, Construction Manager, Logistics Lead, all internal stakeholders)

**Tone:** *one schedule, one critical path, one margin discipline; the OEM and the road upgrades are where the project is won or lost*. Direct, specific, no polish. Internal messages assume technical literacy and commercial awareness.

**Standing message types:**

- **Weekly internal status meeting.** PM-led; Construction Manager, Logistics Lead, Procurement Lead, Engineering Lead, Commercial Manager. 45 minutes. Standing agenda: schedule variance against critical-path chains A–F, cost variance by WBS branch, OEM cadence status, road-upgrade status, risks updated, decisions needed. *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: confirm internal lead assignments per stakeholder register and WBS dictionary])*
- **Daily critical-path huddle during construction.** PM with Construction Manager and Logistics Lead. 15 minutes. Focused on the day's critical-path activities (foundations, erection sequence, component delivery, road-upgrade progress).
- **Monthly portfolio dashboard to Sponsor.** PM produces; Project Director reviews; submitted to portfolio steering. CPI by branch with branch 3.0 (turbine OEM) called out, SPI against energisation critical path, contingency consumed-to-date against $5.5M, top 3 risks, 30-day SC-to-energisation buffer status.
- **Standing-agenda escalation calls.** The OEM concentration risk (R1) and the two-county road coordination risk (R4) are standing agenda items at every internal review until both are de-risked.
- **Internal escalation calls.** Triggered per §4 thresholds.

## 4. Escalation paths and triggers

Escalation follows the charter §11 governance chain: PM → Project Director → Sponsor → CFO. Triggers below are calibrated to Mariposa's contract value ($148M) and commercial structure — larger than Riverside's $42M, so cost-variance thresholds re-size proportionately; tighter than Riverside on schedule because of the hard 30 December year+1 tax-credit deadline.

| Trigger | Escalation level | Time to escalate |
|---|---|---|
| Single-activity schedule slip 7–14 days | PM (no escalation) | — |
| Single-activity schedule slip >14 days | PM → Project Director | Within 48 hours of discovery |
| Critical-path slip >14 days OR projected SC slip | Project Director → Sponsor | Within 24 hours |
| Critical-path slip eroding the 30-day SC-to-energisation buffer | Sponsor → CFO; Client Executive Sponsor briefed in parallel | Same business day |
| Cost variance — single branch >$400k | PM → Project Director | Within 48 hours |
| Cost variance — single branch >$1.0M OR total project >$3.3M (60% of contingency) | Project Director → Sponsor | Within 24 hours |
| Cost variance threatening margin floor (5.7% — full contingency consumed) | Sponsor → CFO (claim-defence and acceleration spend authorisations) | Same business day |
| OEM tranche FAT failure or confirmed delivery slip >4 weeks | PM → Project Director → Sponsor in parallel; Client Lead briefed within 48 hours of Northwood internal position | Within 24 hours internally; client within 48 hours |
| Migratory bird permit excursion or unscheduled blackout extension | PM → Project Director → Sponsor in parallel; permitting authority and client notification per permit | Same business day; regulatory clock starts immediately |
| Either county DOT halts or significantly extends road-upgrade approval | PM → Project Director; Logistics Lead initiates recovery options | Within 48 hours |
| Lost-time injury | PM → Project Director → Sponsor → Client immediately; OSHA notification per regulation | Within hours |
| Press enquiry beyond routine | PM → Project Director → Sponsor; coordinate response with client communications lead | Within hours |
| Organised landowner opposition emerges (e.g., petition, formal complaint group) | PM → Project Director; Sponsor briefed; community-engagement protocol re-confirmed with client | Within 48 hours |
| Utility interconnection study delay or protection-coordination dispute | PM → Project Director; client briefed in parallel | Within 48 hours |

Triggers are not pass-through requests — each escalation is accompanied by a one-page brief stating the event, its commercial and schedule impact (including position against the 30-day SC-to-energisation buffer where relevant), options considered, and the recommended action. The recipient escalates further only if a decision exceeds their authority per charter §11. *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: charter §11 currently flags CFO escalation trigger threshold for confirmation — confirm at kickoff])*

## 6. Crisis / incident communications plan

The standing rhythm in §5 is the project's normal-state communication. The plan below is for breakdown-state events. Four classes of incident are pre-templated for Mariposa, reflecting the project's specific risk profile: the universal long-lead equipment slip and lost-time injury classes, plus the project-specific migratory bird permit non-compliance class and the organised landowner opposition class.

### 6.1 Migratory bird permit non-compliance / wildlife incident

**Trigger.** Confirmed wildlife mortality on site attributable to construction activity; unauthorised work inside a confirmed blackout window; or wildlife agency notice of compliance concern.

**First 4 hours.**
- Construction Manager confirms event and stops work in the affected area.
- PM is briefed within the hour. PM notifies Project Director within the second hour.
- QA/QC Lead initiates incident documentation per permit.

**Hours 4–24.**
- Joint Northwood-client root-cause analysis. QA/QC Lead leads on permit-side; Construction Manager leads on construction-side.
- Client Lead briefed and decides whether to notify the permitting authority within the regulatory window *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: confirm permit notification window with permitting authority once officer assigned])*.
- If notification required: permitting authority notification within the permit window.
- Project Director briefs Sponsor; Sponsor decides on internal portfolio escalation.

**Day 2 onward.**
- Joint corrective action plan agreed with client and permitting authority.
- Permitting authority follow-up submission on the agreed schedule.
- Client Executive Sponsor briefed by Project Director within 48 hours.
- Press protocol: no proactive communication; if press enquires, routed through client communications lead with Northwood factual background.
- Schedule impact assessment: blackout extension or additional mitigation measures may compress Chain D and propagate to energisation. Acceleration options reviewed against contingency.

### 6.2 Long-lead turbine OEM tranche slip or FAT failure

**Trigger.** OEM notifies of delivery slip greater than 4 weeks on any tranche; FAT failure on lead units; or expediter on the ground identifies indicators of >4-week slip risk across the 80-unit fleet.

**First 24 hours.**
- PM briefs Project Director. Procurement Lead initiates formal OEM communication requesting confirmed revised delivery date, root cause, and recovery plan.
- Schedule Engineer models critical-path impact across scenarios on Chains B, E, and F.
- Performance bond status reviewed; escalation rights in supply contract assessed.

**Days 2–5.**
- Northwood-internal review of acceleration options (overtime, additional crews, expedited shipping, parallel erection sequences). Cost-to-accelerate calculated against the cost of slip (LDs to $11.84M cap, tax-credit-tier loss exposure if energisation slips past 30 Dec year+1, claim exposure).
- Sponsor briefed by Project Director with options and recommendation.
- Client Lead briefed by PM within 48 hours of Northwood internal position. Briefing acknowledges the slip honestly and presents Northwood's recovery plan; *does not* anchor the client on the slip as a fixed outcome to energisation.

**Week 2 onward.**
- Acceleration plan executed under Director authority (up to $1M) or Sponsor authority (above); spend tracked against the $1.8M R1 contingency allocation.
- OEM weekly call escalates to daily during recovery period; on-site expediter presence at OEM factory intensified.
- Status report to client includes the slip and the recovery progress in every cycle until resolved.
- If recovery cannot preserve the 30-day SC-to-energisation buffer, Client Executive Sponsor briefed by Project Director; tax-credit-tier conversation initiated with the client.

### 6.3 Lost-time injury

**Trigger.** Any injury resulting in lost work time.

**First 2 hours.**
- Incident response per site safety plan. Medical attention to injured worker as priority.
- HSE Lead notifies PM and Construction Manager immediately.
- PM notifies Project Director within the hour.

**First 24 hours.**
- Project Director notifies Sponsor.
- OSHA notification per regulation (8 hours for hospitalisation, 24 hours for non-hospitalisation lost-time).
- Client Lead notified by PM with factual summary.
- Site safety stand-down within 24 hours; all-hands briefing on incident and immediate corrective measures.
- Camp operations reviewed if incident relates to camp arrangements.

**Week 1 onward.**
- Formal root-cause analysis completed within 7 days.
- HSE plan update within 14 days.
- Client and permitting authority follow-up per their respective procedures.
- Press: no proactive communication; routed through client communications lead if enquiry.

### 6.4 Organised landowner opposition emerges

**Trigger.** Formal petition delivered, complaint group forms with named spokespeople, organised social media campaign, or media coverage of landowner grievance.

**First 24 hours.**
- PM briefs Project Director; Sponsor briefed in parallel.
- Client communications lead briefed; community-engagement protocol re-confirmed with client.
- Stakeholder register §15 rating reviewed for upgrade from Low–Medium to High Influence × High Interest per the contingent flip noted in the register.

**Days 2–7.**
- Joint Northwood-client review of grievance substance. Construction Manager assesses any operational change that could de-escalate (e.g., truck routing, work-hour adjustment).
- Client communications lead leads any direct community engagement; Northwood provides technical background only on request.
- County Roads Leads briefed if grievance involves road impact; county council briefing prepared if council attention likely.
- Press protocol re-confirmed: all enquiries through client communications lead.

**Week 2 onward.**
- Standing community-engagement cadence reviewed; quarterly newsletter cadence may increase to monthly during active opposition *(at draft stage [NEEDS PM REVIEW: cadence change requires formal acknowledgement with client per §5 discipline])*.
- Project Director includes community status in monthly portfolio dashboard until grievance resolves.
- If opposition threatens permit standing or county-council road-upgrade support, escalation per §4 triggers.

## 7. Conventions used

Five patterns this document follows, for traceability against the Northwood worked-example convention:

1. **The communications matrix operationalises the stakeholder register.** Every audience in the matrix corresponds to a stakeholder in the register (§2 of `run07_stakeholder_mariposa.md`). The register identifies *who* and *what engagement strategy*; the matrix specifies *what message*, *what channel*, *what cadence*, *what owner*. Without the register, the matrix has no source-of-truth for audiences.
2. **Audience segmentation drives message-template inventory.** Four segments (client, regulator, community/press, internal). Each segment has a distinct *tone* and three to five *standing message types*. Mixing tones across audiences is a common failure mode that this segmentation prevents.
3. **Escalation triggers are calibrated to the project's contract value and commercial structure.** The Mariposa triggers (single-branch cost variance >$400k, total >$3.3M, 5.7% margin floor) are sized to the $148M contract — proportionately larger than Riverside's $42M, but tighter on schedule because the hard 30 December year+1 tax-credit deadline collapses normal SC-slip tolerance to zero past that date.
4. **Standing rhythm and crisis plan are separate documents within the same plan.** §5 is normal-state; §6 is breakdown-state. Four crisis classes pre-templated for Mariposa: the universal long-lead-slip and LTI classes, plus the project-specific wildlife-permit and organised-opposition classes that reflect this project's risk profile.
5. **Hedging discipline on inferred owners, cadences, and trigger thresholds.** Where any of these has not been confirmed against the charter §11, the stakeholder register, or written client agreement, flagged with the inline italic annotation form. Per-item discipline, not section-wide.

## 8. Notes for downstream agents

- **Status Reporter.** This plan defines the cadence and audience for status reports (monthly written to Client Lead; monthly portfolio dashboard to Sponsor; weekly internal; monthly OEM expediter report; daily critical-path huddle during construction). Status Reporter outputs follow the format committed here. Every cycle from mobilisation onward includes explicit position against the 30-day SC-to-energisation buffer — this is the single most important figure on the project. Crisis-state communications under §6 take precedence over the standing rhythm; a status report following a §6 event includes the event and Northwood's response.
- **Change Order Reviewer.** Change orders flow through the client-side communication channels in §3.1 (Client Lead as the formal channel; Client Executive Sponsor briefed via Project Director on material-commercial-visibility threshold). Client-driven changes affecting energisation date or permitted construction methods require client approval per charter §11 — these are commercial events first, scope events second. The change-order analysis package always includes the marginal impact on the 5.7% margin floor and on the 30-day energisation buffer.
- **Variance Analyst.** Cost and schedule variance reports use the cadences and audiences defined here. Variance events crossing the escalation thresholds in §4 follow the time-to-escalate windows; Variance Analyst's role is to detect and flag, not to communicate — the escalation itself goes through the PM, Project Director, or Sponsor per the trigger. Branch 3.0 (Turbine OEM, 50% of cost base) is the dominant variance-sensitivity branch; any movement here is material.
- **Issue Logger.** Operational issues route into the weekly internal status meeting and the daily critical-path huddle during construction; issues crossing the §4 escalation thresholds get a separate written brief per the trigger. OEM-cadence issues and county-DOT issues are the two issue classes most likely to escalate quickly given the project's risk profile — log with explicit critical-path impact field.
