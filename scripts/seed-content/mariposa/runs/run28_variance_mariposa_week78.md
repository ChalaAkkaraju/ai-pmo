## 6. Variance root cause analysis — final closeout

Three closeout-phase events from Iteration 3 are recorded for completeness (I-029, I-030, I-031); all are below or just at materiality thresholds individually but warrant capture for the audit record and lessons-learned hand-off. Iteration-1 and Iteration-2 root causes (I-021, I-026, I-028) are confirmed at final closeout and cross-referenced; the principal closeout-phase analysis is the **CO-001 closure four-frame retrospective**.

### 6.1 Performance-test blade-pitch calibration drift on 2 of 80 turbines (I-029, Week 73 opened, Week 75 closed; $0 Northwood cost, 0 days schedule)

**Trigger event.** During the 168-hour OEM availability test conducted across the fleet at performance testing, turbines at positions 47 and 62 failed first-attempt due to blade-pitch calibration drift detected during extended operational testing. 78 of 80 turbines (97.5%) passed first attempt — within OEM-stated fleet-level expectation and within Northwood's bid-assumption.

**Decision sequence.** Week 73: OEM availability test surfaces the two failures; OEM mobilises re-calibration team same week under warranty terms negotiated Week 18 (per I-002 closure). Week 75: both turbines re-calibrated, re-tested, and passed availability test on second attempt. Performance bond mechanism not invoked; standard warranty workflow handled the event end-to-end.

**Root cause classification.** **External — vendor (operational acceptance)** per the Northwood six-category scheme. The wear-in calibration drift mode is distinct from I-024's FAT manufacturing-drift mode; it is a normal commissioning-phase tuning event characteristic of operational acceptance, not design or manufacturing fault.

**Lesson learned (confirmed).** Fleet-level performance testing on single-OEM projects above 50 units should budget for ~2–3% first-pass failure on the 168-hour availability test with second-attempt resolution under OEM warranty as the standard pattern — not as an exception. The I-029 event refines the I-024 FAT-discipline lesson by extending "expect findings, time-budget them" from manufacturing acceptance to operational acceptance. **Recommended Northwood standard for single-OEM fleet projects above 50 units: explicit performance-test contingency window of 7–10 working days at the fleet-level availability test stage.**

### 6.2 CO-001 OPCO data-tag mismatch (I-030, Week 65 opened, Week 66 closed; $0.03M absorbed within CO-001 budget tolerance, 0 days schedule)

**Trigger event.** CO-001 OPCO acceptance test first attempt Week 65 revealed data-tag mismatch on 12 of 1,400 mapped tags between the Mariposa SCADA point list and the client OPCO data model.

**Decision sequence.** Week 65: data-tag mismatch surfaced at acceptance test; Northwood and client engineering jointly remediated 12 tags within one week; re-test passed Week 66. Northwood engineering effort <$30k absorbed within Branch 8.0 CO-001 budget tolerance. The CO-001 amendment condition 3 ("data-model snapshot at CO-001 signature condition") was honoured by both parties — Northwood was not liable for changes to the client data model post-snapshot.

**Root cause classification.** **Methodological** — CO-001 scoping was disciplined on the four-frame commercial dynamics but the data-model snapshot mechanism, while explicit in the amendment, did not include a pre-test data-tag reconciliation gate. The remediation cost was modest and absorbed within tolerance; the methodological lesson is preventive rather than corrective.

**Lesson learned (provisional).** For SCADA portfolio-integration scope additions on IPP contracts, a pre-acceptance-test data-tag reconciliation gate (1–2 weeks before formal acceptance test) is a low-cost methodological improvement that would prevent the rework cycle entirely. This is a secondary lesson under Pattern 3 firm-level elevation; the primary lesson remains the four-frame discipline itself.

### 6.3 CO-001 closure four-frame retrospective (executed Week 40, complete Week 65, OPCO acceptance Week 66)

**Final disposition.** CO-001 SCADA portfolio integration: $850k revenue / $780k cost / $70k margin contribution at 8.2%. Schedule-neutral against the binding 30 December year+1 tax-credit deadline. Margin-neutral to base contract economics (base 9.5% bid → post-CO-001 baseline 9.5% → Week 52 compressed 9.3% → Week 78 recovered 9.5%).

**Four-frame retrospective.**

- **Vendor leverage:** Favourable. SCADA vendor offered a standard portfolio-integration module with established pricing and integration playbook — Northwood priced from vendor's standard quote with modest engineering overlay.
- **Client leverage:** Low. Integration was essential to client tax-credit-tier dispatch verification, with no realistic alternative routing through plant-level SCADA alone.
- **Client position:** Strong willingness to pay. Tax-credit-tier economics on client's side dwarfed the $850k integration cost.
- **Northwood acceptance:** Priced at 8.2% margin matching base contract economics (within bid-margin band of 8.0–9.5% used for fixed-price renewables work).

**Root cause classification.** **Client-driven scope addition** per the Northwood six-category scheme. Structurally predicted by portfolio Pattern 3 (client-driven mid-construction scope additions on fixed-price contracts erode margin); the four-frame discipline is the structural prevention.

**Lesson learned (confirmed at closeout for firm-level elevation).** **Mariposa CO-001 at 8.2% realised margin vs Riverside CO-003 pre-discipline 6.7% is the first portfolio-validated renewables-scale empirical anchor for Pattern 3 firm-level standard-practice elevation — a 1.5 percentage-point margin protection on the closed CO event.** A secondary lesson refines the proposal-stage scoping: for tax-credit-tier-verification dispatch integration on IPP projects, proposal-stage scope-statements should explicitly clarify whether SCADA scope ends at plant control or extends to client portfolio OPCO. Asked at proposal, this would have priced the integration into base contract rather than CO, eliminating the 1.3 percentage-point margin compression from Week 52 entirely.
