## 7. Risk register closeout status — framework

Each charter §8 risk has a closeout status. Three categories: **Realised** (the risk event occurred during execution), **Mitigated** (the risk was actively managed and did not realise), **Not materialised** (the risk did not appear despite being live). Each carries cited evidence — contingency consumption, variance section, change-order ID, or issue-log reference.

| R3 — Migratory bird flyway blackout periods | *(Realised / Mitigated / Not materialised)* | *(blackout window confirmation outcome from I-003 closure; Chain D sequencing impact; contingency consumption against $700k R3 allocation)* |
| R4 — Rural-road upgrades across two counties | *(Realised / Mitigated / Not materialised)* | *(Chain C completion narrative; per-county DOT engagement outcomes; contingency consumption against $800k R4 allocation)* |
| R5 — Rural labour market / camp accommodation | *(Realised / Mitigated / Not materialised)* | *(camp build-vs-lease decision outcome from I-004 closure; productivity trajectory across construction; contingency consumption against $500k R5 allocation)* |

**Closeout categorisation discipline.** The three-category framing prevents the "all risks managed successfully" closeout summary. A risk that did not realise because the mitigation worked is **Mitigated**; a risk that did not appear at all despite being live is **Not materialised**. The distinction matters for firm-level lessons — mitigated risks validate the mitigation; not-materialised risks return contingency without validating anything.

## 10. Conventions used

Five patterns this document follows, for traceability against the Northwood worked-example convention (Skyhawk Solar + Storage Phase 1 closeout):

1. **Objective-by-objective binary status with quantitative supporting metrics.** Charter §3's five objectives are the report's spine; each gets a Met / Partially met / Not met status at headline level with quantitative detail in supporting columns. A closeout report that buries objective performance in narrative fails its primary audience (client and Sponsor). The binary status format is the discipline that surfaces outcome at headline level. Objective 2 (energisation by 30 December year+1) is binary without a partial-met state because the federal tax-credit-tier outcome is binary on the client side.

2. **Risk register closeout status with three categories.** Realised / Mitigated / Not materialised, with cited evidence (contingency consumption per budget §4 allocations, variance section reference, change-order ID, or issue-log closure). The three-category framing prevents the "all risks managed successfully" closeout summary that obscures which risks actually appeared and which did not. The distinction between mitigated and not-materialised matters for firm-level lessons.

3. **Audience-aware disclosure discipline.** §1 Executive summary is the client-and-Sponsor-facing headline; §3 Financial summary is full-detail for Sponsor and portfolio dashboard with no branch-level detail to client beyond contractually required disclosure; §8 Lessons learned quotes only headlines from the synthesis with the full synthesis internal-only unless client-facing lessons are pre-approved. The disclosure boundary is operational, not editorial.

4. **Lessons-learned cross-reference, not duplication.** §8 quotes headline lessons from the companion Lessons-Learned Synthesis; the synthesis carries the full content with evidence base and firm-level recommendations. The two documents are bidirectional cross-references — closeout report cites synthesis sections; synthesis cites closeout outcome metrics including risk register closeout status §7 as a primary evidence source for risk-themed lessons.

5. **Follow-on and handover items separated from outcome items.** §9 collects operational handover, client-relationship continuation (Phase 2 prospect), portfolio-level transfer, and documentation archive items — forward-looking dimensions distinct from the outcome reporting in §1–§8. The separation makes the operational handover actionable and prevents forward-looking items from contaminating the outcome record. Forward-looking items dependent on events not yet occurred (Phase 2 prospect, warranty events, follow-on adoption status) carry inline italic hedging.
