## 3. Notes for PM

**Risks considered and excluded from the Northwood-side register, with rationale:**

- **Settlement monitoring at remediated positions 16/23/41 as a standalone risk** — folded into R-009 trigger rather than carried separately; remediated positions will be monitored through commissioning per standard foundation-acceptance ITP, and any anomaly reopens R-009. Discrete entry would duplicate the trigger language without adding management content.
- **CO-001 SCADA portfolio integration delivery risk (integration workstream 50% complete, due Week 65)** — excluded because the workstream is on track and the commercial structure is margin-neutral; integration is execution of confirmed scope, not residual risk. Reopen if Week 60 progress review shows slip beyond two weeks against Week 65 target.
- **Remaining-tranche OEM delivery (post-Week 50, manufacturing complete)** — folded into R-001 residual exposure rather than separate entry; all deliveries cadence-tracked through Week 50 per the events brief, no further tranches anticipated. R-001 retains residual probability against warranty-tail commitments.
- **Erection-pace recovery to 1.7/week by Week 60** — not a standalone risk; folded into R-002 trigger condition. The pace shortfall is a known recovery requirement managed through I-027 lessons (sequence optimisation, crane re-positioning); if recovery fails by Week 60 the R-002 buffer-erosion trigger fires.
- **Commissioning-stage utility coordination on energisation event** — folded into R-008 residual trigger rather than separate entry; the interconnection agreement is signed and protection-coordination is complete, so the remaining exposure is operational coordination at energisation rather than a discrete commercial risk.

**Risks that belong on the client's side of the table (per rule 9):**

- **Environmental permitting (avian study extension or amendment)** — client-owned per charter §4.2; Northwood-side exposure managed through R-003 monitoring of Officer Whitlock interactions, not as a Northwood risk.
- **Land lease management and landowner relationships** — client-owned per charter §4.2; the R-012 stakeholder risk on organised opposition is a Northwood operational concern through community-engagement support, but contractual exposure on land-lease integrity is client-side.
- **Transmission line beyond point of interconnection** — utility-owned per charter §4.2; R-008 has now mitigated for Northwood's interconnection-study dependency and does not extend to line availability.
- **PPA commercial terms and tax-credit-tier qualification mechanics** — client-owned; Northwood's commitment is the 30 December year+1 energisation date per R-002. The CO-001 SCADA portfolio integration is the dispatch-verification enabler; mechanism qualification remains with the client and their tax counsel.
- **Client OPCO readiness for SCADA portfolio integration acceptance at Week 65 mechanical completion** — client-side acceptance dependency on CO-001 integration; tracked through Commercial Manager interface, not as a Northwood risk.
