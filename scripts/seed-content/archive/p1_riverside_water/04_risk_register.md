## Lessons logged from this register

1. **Single-sourced critical-path equipment is a category-level risk.** R-001 alone consumed almost the entire project contingency. Northwood should require dual-source qualification for any critical-path equipment with lead time greater than 6 months.
2. **Plan B sequencing exercises before the schedule is in trouble.** The Plan B commissioning sequence was conceived only after R-001 triggered. Future projects of similar scale should war-game alternative commissioning sequences during Engineering, not under pressure during Construction.
3. **Currency hedging is consistently profitable.** Forward contracts on R-010 contributed positively to margin protection. Should be standard for any EUR/JPY exposure greater than $1M.
