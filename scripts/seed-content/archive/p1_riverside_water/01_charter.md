# Project Charter — Riverside Water Reclamation Phase 2 Expansion

## 1. Project identification

- **Project name:** Riverside Water Reclamation — Phase 2 Expansion
- **Project ID:** NW-WW-2103
- **Client:** City of Riverside Public Works Department
- **Contract type:** Fixed-price
- **Contract value:** $42.0 million
- **Effective date:** 12 March
- **Target substantial completion date:** 30 January, year+2
- **Sponsor (Northwood):** VP Water & Wastewater
- **Project Director:** R. Tannehill
- **Project Manager:** M. Acharya

## 2. Overview

Expansion of the City of Riverside's existing 18 MGD wastewater treatment facility to a treated capacity of 30 MGD. Northwood will design, procure, build and commission additional headworks screening, two new biological treatment trains, two secondary clarifiers, tertiary filtration improvements, and the associated electrical, instrumentation and control systems. Project enables the city to meet projected demand through year+15 and to satisfy a state-mandated effluent standard taking effect in three years.

## 3. Objectives

1. Deliver treated capacity of 30 MGD, verified through 30-day continuous performance test, by 30 January year+2.
2. Achieve effluent compliance with the new state standard (TN <8 mg/L, TP <0.5 mg/L) verified during the performance test.
3. Maintain plant operations throughout construction with no unplanned process upsets exceeding state-permit thresholds.
4. Deliver the project at or above 8.5% margin on contract value, with cost variance within ±3% of approved budget.
5. Achieve zero lost-time injuries across the construction period.

## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In scope
- Detailed design of all civil, mechanical, electrical and I&C work packages.
- Procurement of all process equipment, including aeration blowers, clarifier mechanisms, filtration media, and SCADA upgrades.
- Construction of two new biological treatment basins, two new secondary clarifiers, headworks expansion, and tertiary filter retrofit.
- Tie-ins to existing process trains during scheduled plant shutdowns.
- Commissioning, performance testing, operator training, and as-built documentation.
- 24-month warranty period.

### 4.2 Out of scope
- Sludge dewatering capacity expansion (client deferred to a future phase).
- Effluent outfall modifications (separate contract awarded to a marine specialist).
- Headworks grit removal equipment replacement (client-supplied, installed by client's O&M team).
- Plant administration building improvements.

## 9. Assumptions

1. Existing plant remains in continuous operation throughout construction.
2. Permitted shutdown windows align with seasonal flow patterns as historically observed.
3. Client-supplied grit removal equipment is delivered to site no later than 30 June year+1.
4. State regulator's performance test procedure remains as agreed in proposal.

## 10. Constraints

1. Plant must maintain effluent compliance throughout construction.
2. Construction noise limited to daytime hours by city ordinance.
3. State-mandated effluent standard takes effect on 30 March year+2 — no schedule slip possible past this date without permit consequence.
