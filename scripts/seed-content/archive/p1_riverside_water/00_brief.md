# Riverside Water Reclamation — Phase 2 Expansion

**Project ID:** NW-WW-2103
**Status at archive:** Closed, handed over Q4 last fiscal year, 7 months into warranty.
**Contract value:** $42 million, fixed-price.
**Duration:** 22 months from Award to Substantial Completion. Substantial Completion delivered 6 weeks late.
**Final margin:** 4.1% (against bid margin of 8.5%).

## What the project did

Capacity expansion of the Riverside Municipal Water Reclamation Facility — a wastewater treatment plant serving a city of ~180,000 — from 18 MGD to 30 MGD treatment capacity, with associated headworks, biological treatment trains, secondary clarifiers, and tertiary filtration upgrades.

## Why margin compressed

Two main causes. A 14-week delay in delivery of two large blowers from a single-sourced European vendor, which absorbed almost all the project's contingency. And a late client-driven change order (Q2 of construction) adding UV disinfection capacity that was negotiated at thin margin. Liquidated damages were assessed for the late completion but were partially offset by a successful claim for client-driven scope additions.

## Outcomes

- Performance tests passed first attempt.
- Client relationship intact; Northwood was shortlisted for the Phase 3 work.
- Lessons-learned write-up flagged single-sourced critical equipment as a category-level risk for the firm.
