# Ironvale Smelter Modernisation

**Project ID:** NW-IND-2110
**Status at archive:** Closed, handed over 14 months ago. Warranty completed.
**Contract value:** $26.5 million, T&M with cap.
**Duration:** 11 months Award to Mechanical Completion.
**Final margin:** 12.3% (against bid margin of 10%).

## What the project did

Modernisation of a 35-year-old secondary aluminium smelter — replacement of two furnace lines, full re-instrumentation, SCADA upgrade, environmental controls (baghouse, scrubber), and tie-ins to existing crucible casting bays. Client is a regional industrial group; smelter feeds their own downstream extrusion business.

## Why this one outperformed

T&M contract structure with a cap, which suited a project where scope evolved through engineering. Strong client engagement — their plant engineer was effectively embedded with the Northwood team. Existing site conditions were unusually well-documented (the client had digitised plant records in the previous two years). No major scope changes after Engineering 60% review.

## Notable

Significant existing-condition risks (the original smelter was older than parts of Northwood's risk catalogue assumed). Two latent defects discovered during demolition — both absorbed through the cap structure with client cost-share. Scheduling around plant production windows was tight but the client gave Northwood a full plant shutdown for the final tie-in week, which was the right call.
