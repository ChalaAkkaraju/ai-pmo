# Project Charter — Ironvale Smelter Modernisation

## 1. Project identification

- **Project name:** Ironvale Smelter Modernisation
- **Project ID:** NW-IND-2110
- **Client:** Ironvale Metals Group
- **Contract type:** T&M with cap ($30M cap)
- **Contract value:** $26.5 million (working value at Award)
- **Effective date:** 14 August
- **Target Mechanical Completion:** 30 June, year+1
- **Sponsor (Northwood):** VP Industrial
- **Project Director:** S. Friedman
- **Project Manager:** P. Beaumont

## 2. Overview

Modernisation of an operating secondary aluminium smelter to extend plant life by 20 years and bring emissions performance within the latest state air-quality limits. Northwood replaces two of the three furnace lines, upgrades the SCADA and instrumentation system end-to-end, installs new baghouse and scrubber for environmental control, and ties the new lines into the existing crucible casting bays.

## 3. Objectives

1. Replace Furnace Lines A and B with new technology, verified through 14-day production performance test, by 30 June year+1.
2. Achieve emissions compliance with state Air Quality Rule 27 (effective immediately at restart) verified during commissioning.
3. Maintain client production output on the unaffected Furnace Line C throughout construction (no shutdown of C beyond planned tie-in week).
4. Deliver at or above 10% margin with cost variance within ±5% (T&M cap rules).
5. Zero lost-time injuries.

## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In scope
- Detailed design of furnace lines A and B, SCADA, baghouse, scrubber.
- Procurement of furnaces, baghouse modules, scrubber, motor control centres, instrumentation, control system hardware.
- Demolition of existing Furnace Lines A and B.
- Construction and installation of new lines, environmental controls, SCADA upgrade.
- Tie-ins to existing crucible bays and ancillary services.
- Commissioning, performance testing, operator training, as-builts.
- 12-month warranty (shorter than standard due to T&M structure).

### 4.2 Out of scope
- Furnace Line C (not part of this scope).
- Casting bay equipment.
- Site civil works beyond the furnace bay perimeter.
- Permits (client).

## 9. Assumptions

1. Existing site documentation reflects current as-installed conditions (Ironvale digitised records 2 years ago).
2. Client provides agreed 7-day shutdown window in June year+1.
3. Permits remain valid through Mechanical Completion.

## 10. Constraints

1. Furnace Line C must remain operational throughout construction except during the agreed shutdown window.
2. State Air Quality Rule 27 effective immediately at restart of Lines A and B.
