## Lessons logged

1. **Embedded client SME is a multiplier.** T. Hartnett's daily presence accelerated decisions and reduced rework. Northwood should ask for this on every modernisation project where the client has the relevant expertise.
2. **T&M with a cap is the right contract structure for projects with high latent-condition risk.** Aligns incentives, allows scope to evolve in Engineering, contains client exposure. Should be a default consideration on facility-modernisation work.
3. **A full plant shutdown is sometimes the cheapest path.** The client's willingness to give us a full week was the single largest schedule contributor. Future modernisation bids should make the cost-benefit case for full shutdown openly with the client.
