## 2. Overview

EPC delivery of a 200 MW solar photovoltaic generation facility with a co-located 100 MWh / 4-hour lithium-iron-phosphate battery energy storage system in the desert southwest. Northwood designs, procures and constructs the PV array, BESS, substation, MV collection system, and 230 kV grid interconnection. Client operates under a 20-year power purchase agreement with the regional utility.

## 3. Objectives

1. Deliver 200 MW DC nameplate solar generation, verified through commissioning power test, by 5 June year+1.
2. Deliver 100 MWh BESS with ≥95% round-trip efficiency at commissioning, sustained.
3. Achieve grid interconnection energisation by 1 May year+1 to enable 35-day commissioning window.
4. Deliver at or above 8.8% margin with cost variance within ±2% of approved budget.
5. Zero lost-time injuries across construction.

## 4. Scope

### 4.1 In scope
- Detailed design of PV, BESS, substation, MV collection, interconnection.
- Procurement of modules, inverters, BESS containers, transformer, switchgear, racking.
- Construction including grading, foundations, tracker installation, BESS pad, MV trenching, substation construction, fencing, access roads.
- Commissioning, performance testing, training, as-builts.
- 24-month warranty.

### 4.2 Out of scope
- Land acquisition (client).
- Environmental permitting (client; Northwood supports).
- 230 kV transmission line from substation to grid POI (separate utility-led contract).
- O&M services post handover.
