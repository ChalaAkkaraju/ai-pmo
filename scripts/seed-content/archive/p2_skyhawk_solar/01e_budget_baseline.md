## 6. Cost reporting framework

### 6.1 Variance reporting cadence

- **Weekly internal**: cost-to-complete forecast vs P50 baseline, by WBS branch. Variance >2% on any branch flagged for review.
- **Monthly client status**: invoiced-vs-baseline at milestone level; no branch-level disclosure to client.
- **Monthly portfolio**: cumulative cost variance, contingency consumed-to-date, contingency-remaining vs. risks-remaining.

### 6.2 Earned value method

The project uses cost-plus-progress earned-value calculations:

- BCWP (Budgeted Cost of Work Performed) at each branch, calculated from progress percentages reported by the engineering, procurement, and construction leads.
- ACWP (Actual Cost of Work Performed) from invoiced and accrued cost data.
- BCWS (Budgeted Cost of Work Scheduled) from the schedule baseline.
- CPI (Cost Performance Index) = BCWP / ACWP. Target ≥ 1.00. CPI < 0.95 on any branch triggers PM-Director conversation; CPI < 0.90 triggers re-baseline review.

### 6.3 Re-baseline thresholds

- Single-branch overrun exceeding $200k OR exceeding 10% of branch P50, whichever is smaller: cost update, no re-baseline.
- Single-branch overrun exceeding $500k OR exceeding 20% of branch P50: re-baseline that branch with rationale; change-order if client-driven, internal-only if Northwood-driven.
- Total project cost overrun exceeding $1.5M (60% of contingency) OR margin projection dropping below 5.1% (full-contingency-consumed floor): portfolio escalation per charter §11 governance; immediate Sponsor brief.

Cost-baseline-integrity owner: J. Okafor (PM) with Commercial Manager support. Weekly cost reviews include the Construction Manager, Procurement Lead, and Engineering Lead.

## 8. Conventions used

Five patterns this document follows, for traceability against the Northwood worked-example convention:

1. **Cost baseline as P50 plus separately-tracked contingency.** The P50 budget ($62.0M) is the operational target. The $2.5M contingency is tracked separately. Combining them obscures both the operating-cost discipline and the risk-driven contingency reasoning.
2. **Decomposition by WBS branch with confidence bands.** Each Level-2 WBS branch carries a P50 cost and a confidence band (Tight / Medium). Branches with Medium bands drive the contingency-allocation reasoning. The decomposition makes the cost baseline auditable against the WBS scope.
3. **Contingency allocation reasoned against the risk register.** The $2.5M contingency is mapped to the five charter §8 risks (R1–R5) plus an unallocated reserve. This is the analytical view; the operational view is that the PM holds the $2.5M and may re-allocate. Re-allocation within the envelope is PM discretion; expansion beyond requires Director approval.
4. **Cash flow tied to milestone schedule with net working capital tracked at each milestone.** The milestone-by-milestone cash projection is the basis for treasury planning and for catching the "low point" of net working capital before it becomes a problem.
5. **Re-baseline thresholds in dollars and percentages, not judgement calls.** Single-branch and total-project thresholds are explicit. PM, Director, and Sponsor each have a clear escalation trigger.
