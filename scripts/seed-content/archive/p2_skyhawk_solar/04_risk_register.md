## Lessons logged

1. **Utility interconnection is a perennial schedule risk.** Recurred from Riverside (where it was different — state regulator inspection — but same pattern). PMO standard should be earliest possible engagement with all external dependency owners.
2. **Dual-sourcing critical equipment paid off again.** Even though primary source delivered, the qualified secondary gave us pricing leverage and accelerated-delivery options if needed.
3. **Weather window management is a Day-1 discipline, not a recovery technique.** Two of the four monsoon lost-day events were avoidable with better look-ahead — fed back into the firm's weather-management standard.
