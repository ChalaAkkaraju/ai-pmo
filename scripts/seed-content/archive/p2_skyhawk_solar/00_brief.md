# Skyhawk Solar + Storage — Phase 1

**Project ID:** NW-REN-2207
**Status at archive:** Closed, handed over 9 months ago. End of warranty in 15 months.
**Contract value:** $68 million, fixed-price.
**Duration:** 14 months Award to Substantial Completion. Delivered on schedule.
**Final margin:** 9.1% (against bid margin of 8.8%).

## What the project did

200 MW utility-scale solar PV array with co-located 100 MWh / 4-hour lithium-iron-phosphate battery energy storage system, including substation, MV collection system, and grid interconnection at 230 kV. Client is an independent power producer; offtake under a 20-year PPA with a regional utility.

## Why this one went well

Standard, mature technology stack. Northwood had delivered three similar solar projects in the previous two fiscal years; team continuity was high. Vendor relationships were established. Weather window was managed actively from Day 1 of construction. Battery storage was the only novel component; risk-managed via early factory acceptance testing of the BESS containers.

## Notable risks managed

Interconnection studies came in late from the utility and threatened to delay commissioning by 6 weeks. Recovered by reordering punch-list work and adding a second commissioning crew for two weeks. Cost impact: $180k overtime, absorbed within contingency.
